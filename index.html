About the cabin

Rustic comfort with a story rooted in nature.

Nestled on the Chogoria side of Mt. Kenya, the cabin is a quiet highland hideaway designed for comfort, rest, and connection with the natural world. Whether you are traveling solo, as a couple, or in a group, you will enjoy the wide open views, fresh air, and warm welcome.

The surrounding area is alive with birds, trees, and mountain energy — making each morning feel calm, restorative, and unforgettable.
