The cabin

A quiet mountain hideaway with warm local hospitality.

Set on the cool slopes above Chogoria, our rustic cabin is designed for rest, reflection, and easy connection with nature. The mountain air, lush gardens, warm interiors, and peaceful atmosphere create a memorable stay for every guest.

Whether you’re looking to enjoy birdwatching, relax with a good view, prepare a meal in the kitchen, or simply slow down away from city noise, Chogoria Cabin gives you the space to breathe deeply and feel at home.
