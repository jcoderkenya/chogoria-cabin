Unforgettable Experiences

Birdwatching, Safaris & Farm Tours

From the garden balcony to Mount Kenya's highest trails, adventure is right on your doorstep.

Birdwatcher's Sanctuary

Spot vivid local birdlife right from the balcony: D'Arnaud's Barbet, Woodland Kingfishers, African Hoopoes, Little Bee-eaters, Common Bulbuls, and Hamerkops.

Spot right from the veranda →

Mount Kenya Chogoria Route

Chogoria is celebrated as the most scenic approach to Mount Kenya. Use the cabin as your base before ascending to Lake Ellis, Gorges Valley, and Point Lenana.

Premier trekking trailhead →

Samburu & Meru Safaris

Convenient day excursions into Meru National Park (home of Elsa the lioness) and Samburu National Reserve for elephant and big cat sightings.

Game drive access →

Tea & Coffee Farm Tours

Walk through emerald tea plantations and coffee estates. Experience local factory processing and taste highland Kenyan coffee at its origin.

Agro-tourism experience →

Obadiah's Farm & Local Life

Visit the working farm right next door. Harvest fresh vegetables, learn about tropical fruits, and chat with Obadiah about local history and folklore.

Next door to cabin →

Cycling, Walks & Boda Boda

Gentle country walks, cycling along dirt tracks, and colorful strolls through Chogoria market followed by a fun Boda Boda ride back up to the cabin.
